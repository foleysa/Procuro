**Date:** May 3, 2026
**Goal:** Replace "Procuro" with a name that's clear of trademark and brand conflicts in procurement / spend / B2B SaaS, with usable .com (or strong alternates) and free social handles.

> ⚠️ **Caveat:** All checks below are public web scans (search-based reads of company/domain/handle footprints, not direct WHOIS or USPTO TESS queries). Treat as a clearance signal, not a legal opinion. Final pick should be confirmed by counsel and a real WHOIS lookup before purchase.

## Signal legend

- 🟢 **GREEN** — No direct namesake found in target space; .com or strong alternate appears free; social handles unindexed.
- 🟡 **YELLOW** — A namesake exists but in an unrelated category, or .com is taken while alternates are open. Usable with care.
- 🔴 **RED** — Direct namesake in procurement / spend / B2B SaaS, OR a unicorn-scale brand on the same word, OR phonetic collision with an established competitor. Avoid.

## My recommended top 4 to consider

Ranked by strongest signal of being clear AND fitting the "procurement-as-a-service / contingency-savings" story:

1. **CashLever** 🟢 — On-message (your whole thesis is "Tier-1 levers that move cash"), short, two real English words so it's immediately understood, and fully clear across web/USPTO/social. Strongest brand-meaning fit.
2. **SpendKite** 🟢 — Distinctive in the "Spend___" naming pattern (vs. SpendHQ / Spendflo / Spendkey), evokes lift and steering. Fully clear.
3. **Atlas Procure** 🟢 — Classic, trustworthy, easy to say. Adjacent consultancies exist but no SaaS competitor. Reads more "enterprise" than "scrappy."
4. **Procyl** 🟡 — Short, coined, clean in software/SaaS. Trade-off: .com is held by a pharma company, so you'd launch on procyl.io / procyl.ai. Most "brandable" of the invented names.

**Avoid:** Klera, Vendra, Cinch, Frugl, MarginIQ — each has a real competitor or near-collision in spend/procurement/finance SaaS.

**Soft no:** Suvio (consumer AI app on .ai dilutes), SavingsLoop (long, slightly literal), Vantage Procure (NPI Vantage Pro adjacency in IT procurement is a watch-item).
