## My top 3 from round 2

1. **Lodara** 🟢 — The cleanest invented name I've scanned across both rounds. Nothing in any startup DB, no domain conflicts, no social handle conflicts. "Lode" (a vein of valuable ore) plus a soft "-ara" ending — feels like a serious B2B brand (think Aldara, Versara) and tells a quiet story about finding value veins in spend.
2. **Provent** 🟢 — Sounds the most like an enterprise procurement brand on the list. Hints at "provide" + "prevent waste" without being literal. Industrial-ventilation companies use it on adjacent domains, but **no software competitor anywhere**. Defensible in classes 9/35/42.
3. **Margi** 🟢 — Short, warm, on-message clip of "margin." Easy to say, fintech-feel. Riskier than the other two because 5-letter common-stem names can be tough to defend at trademark level, but the procurement/SaaS space is clear.
